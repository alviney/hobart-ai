- Personal daily digest (now being explored as the home-screen briefing in the prototype): a user sets up their own scheduled notification, driven by a prompt or pipeline. It can format their banked-up (below-threshold) notifications, read from their own "store" (tables they push data into from other workflows, for advanced users), and fetch outside content ("top 3 breaking tech news stories, a few lines each"). Delivered like any other notification. Each run makes AI calls, so it's a likely paid-tier feature (pricing in Q-003).
- "Scan for live updates" QR codes: a publisher (a shop, café or club) downloads a printable QR poster or counter card for their channel. Scanning it opens a one-tap subscribe page (pick a threshold, enter an email or connect your AI). It's the channel's open invite link as a QR code, so it builds on notify-core's link invites. It also brings in subscribers without the publisher needing their contacts, and puts the brand in physical places around Hobart (see marketing/). Open question for the owner when scoped: can anyone who scans join, or does the publisher approve each one?
