# D-008: Working name Sub1; entities are Troves, messages are Pings

- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Status:** accepted
- **Answers:** Q-001
- **Projects:** [notify-core](../projects/notify-core.md), [ui-mockups](../projects/ui-mockups.md), [pilot-publishers](../projects/pilot-publishers.md)

## Context

The product needed a name (Q-001) and plain words for its two core things.
The owner is leaning towards **Sub1**, with submarine and sonar branding.

## Decision

- Working product name: **Sub1**. It's a working name until the owner has
  checked the domain and trademark (see "Only you can do").
- An entity (a club, school, shop, group, street) is a **Trove**. Anyone
  can start one (D-006).
- A message from a Trove is a **Ping**. People **subscribe** to Troves.
- Branding leans into sonar and submarines: a sonar mark, a radar screen,
  and "strong" and "weak" pings.

Words from earlier docs map across: channel or entity = Trove, update or
notification = Ping, connections = Your Troves.

## Options considered

- **Sub1 with Troves and Pings**: short, distinctive, and the sonar
  metaphor explains ping strength and the radar without extra words.
- **A Hobart-specific name**: rejected earlier (Q-001 recommendation); it
  limits growth beyond Hobart.

## Consequences

Marketing drafts in `marketing/` still say "channel" and "update"; update
them when they're next touched. Revisit the name if the domain or
trademark check fails.
