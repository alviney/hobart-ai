# D-003: Marketing sells the dream and makes people feel early and capable

- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Status:** accepted
- **Projects:** [pilot-publishers](../projects/pilot-publishers.md)

## Context

The owner wants marketing to do two things. First, sell the end dream (a
live, AI-connected Hobart), not just a notifications tool. Second, make the
average person feel this is the new way of doing things and they should get
on board, while feeling empowered and excited to have something that until
now only frontier techies had, by stitching together self-hosted agent
setups (the OpenClaw kind).

## Decision

All marketing follows [marketing/positioning.md](../../marketing/positioning.md):
sell the vision, make people feel early rather than left behind, and frame
the product as giving everyday people a capability that used to need a
homelab. Claims about what the product does today must be true today.

## Options considered

- **Vision-led, empowering**: gives early adopters a reason to join before
  the network is big, and suits a product whose value grows with use.
- **Feature-led ("a better group notifier")**: easy to understand, but it's
  a commodity pitch and doesn't explain why to switch from WhatsApp.

## Consequences

Copy needs a clear line between "today" and "where we're going", or it will
overpromise. Revisit if early users say they feel pressured or confused
about what the product actually does.
