How it works

One feed for everything local

Every club, school, shop and street group can run a Station. You subscribe to the ones you care about, and their updates arrive as pings.

Find your Stations

Scan the code at the school gate or café counter, accept an invite, or see what's near you.

Choose what reaches you

Everything, or urgent only. A closure pings you. A croissant update waits in your feed.

Ask, don't scroll

"What's on this weekend?" Get an answer from the Stations you follow, in the app or in your own AI.

The radar

Walk past. Tune in.

Turn on the radar and Sub1 shows the Stations around you and what they've said lately. Standing at the school, the market or a new café? It's right there at the top, one tap to subscribe.

  • Fresh pings shine; old news fades, so you know what's current.
  • Search what's nearby: "good bagels", "kids this weekend".
  • Your location stays on your phone.
